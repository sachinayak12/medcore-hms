# 1. Business Problem

Many hospitals use separate systems for registration, appointments, billing and pharmacy. This causes duplicate data, scheduling conflicts and poor coordination between departments.

**MedCore HMS** aims to provide one centralized platform for all hospital operations.

---

# 2. Business Objective

The system should:

- Register and manage patients
- Schedule appointments
- Maintain electronic medical records
- Generate bills
- Manage pharmacy inventory
- Keep complete audit history

---

# 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Responsibility |
|------------|----------------|
| Receptionist | Patient registration & appointments |
| Doctor | Consultation & prescriptions |
| Lab Technician | Upload lab reports |
| Pharmacist | Medicine dispensing |
| Administrator | User management & reports |
| Patient | View appointments & reports |

---

# 4. Project Scope

## In Scope (MVP)

- Patient Management
- Appointment Scheduling
- Doctor Consultation
- Billing
- User Authentication
- Audit Logs

## Out of Scope

- Insurance claims
- Online payment gateway
- Mobile application
- Telemedicine

# 6. Success Metrics

| KPI | Target |
|------|--------|
| Registration Time | < 2 minutes |

---

# 7. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Double booking | Slot validation |
| Duplicate patients | Unique phone number |
| Unauthorized access | JWT + RBAC |
| Slow dashboard | Redis cache |
